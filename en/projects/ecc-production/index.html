Scope of the work

The project began with paper manufacturing cycles whose records were difficult to consolidate and review. I developed an Apps Script web application with connected spreadsheet tables, bringing daily operations and management views into one environment.

The interface moves from weighing, manufacturing and laboratory activity to individual mixer performance, product analysis and downtime. Operational records can therefore be read in context rather than as isolated entries.

My role in the project

Workflow analysis, application development and data modelling

01

Daily operations

Weighing, manufacturing and laboratory sections, with current work separated from completed records and archives.

02

Equipment performance

Per-mixer views of total batches, operating hours, average waste and recorded faults.

03

Analysis & supervision

Views for product manufacturing time, waste and downtime, alongside efficiency monitoring, employee performance and an issue log.

The workflow

  1. 01Record and review weighing, manufacturing and laboratory data.
  2. 02Consolidate batches, operating hours, waste and recorded faults by mixer.
  3. 03Review product performance and downtime through analysis views.
  4. 04Refer back to records and reports to follow the production cycle.
