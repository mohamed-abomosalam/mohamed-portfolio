Clinic Management System

Dr. Mohamed Abdel Baky Clinic / Responsive web app

A clinic operations system designed for mobile screens. It brings together booking, patient records, medical examinations and prescriptions, with finance, stock and settings in one Arabic interface.

PROJECT CONTEXT

Dr. Mohamed Abdel Baky Clinic / Responsive web app

OUTCOME

A responsive booking and clinic-record workflow on mobile.

Actual project interfaces. Select a capture and enlarge it to explore the details.

ScopeWorkflowTools & outcomeRelated work

Scope of the work

I built the clinic experience around fast access to daily tasks on a phone: book an appointment, find a registered patient, then move to the patient record, examination and prescription.

The booking screen separates new and registered patients, with visit type and payment status. The navigation groups patient records, examinations, prescriptions, finance, stock and settings.

My role in the project

Application design, development & mobile experience

01

Booking & reception

A phone-oriented form brings booking data, visit type and payment status into clear steps.

02

Clinical workflow

Dedicated sections for patient records, examinations and prescriptions.

03

Clinic administration

Finance, stock and settings are accessible through navigation that adapts to a narrow screen.

The workflow

  1. 01Choose a new patient or find a registered patient.
  2. 02Enter booking information, visit type and payment status.
  3. 03Move between the patient record, examination and prescription.
  4. 04Access finance, stock and settings.
