Hafa Store — Retail Management

Hafa Store / Sales, inventory & accounts

An integrated system for mobile-phone retail operations. It starts with point-of-sale and barcode lookup, then brings together invoices, instalments and financial transfers with inventory counts, reports and profits.

Scope of the work

I designed the system around the actual sale: choose an item, enter quantity and discount, and review the total before completing the transaction. The interface supports barcode lookup as well as manual item selection.

The operating menu brings together invoices, instalments, financial transfers, item creation, barcode printing, inventory counts and reports. The sales screen also provides optional customer details and a WhatsApp invoice action.

The workflow

  1. 01Look up a barcode or select an item manually.
  2. 02Set quantity and discount, then review the total.
  3. 03Complete the sale and send the invoice through WhatsApp.
  4. 04Follow invoices, instalments, inventory counts and reports.
